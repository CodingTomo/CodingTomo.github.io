# So, Why a Blog?

So, hey. I figured I'd start a little corner here to jot down stuff that happens in my day-to-day life. Mostly it'll be work and math stuff, since that's what fills up most of my brain, but honestly I'll probably ramble about whatever else feels worth sharing too.

No fixed schedule, no content calendar, nothing like that. I'll post when I actually have something to say and the time to sit down and write it out.

Only promise I'm making: I'll actually try to write this stuff myself, not just let an LLM do all the talking.
