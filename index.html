Hi, I'm Tomo

I'm a Data Scientist at Verizon Connect in Florence. I kicked things off at Target Reply in Milan before coming back here to get my Ph.D. in Machine Learning working at the Global Optimization Laboratory. I'm sticking around Florence and now work at Verizon. My background is a mix of Math and Computer Engineering. These days, I'm really into computer vision, intelligent vehicle applications, and multimodal models. In the past, I've also spent time working on continual learning and sustainable AI.
